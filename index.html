About

Video Editor

I'm a Video Editor focused on visual storytelling and precise pacing.

I work on video projects for social media, ads and music videos, making sure every cut and shot serves the story, not just the look.

I'm Youssef, a video editor for a few years now, always picking up new tools and techniques. I work on social media, advertising and music video projects, mainly using Premiere Pro, After Effects and DaVinci Resolve. I care a lot about keeping the work clean, organized and fast to deliver, and I simplify my workflow as much as I can so I can focus on the creative part instead of technical overhead. My current goal is to specialize more in Motion Design and bring AI tools into my workflow so I can produce stronger content in less time.
